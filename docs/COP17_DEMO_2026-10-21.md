## Implemented scope

- Settings expose ANEMONE eDNA and Satellite SST; advanced model/retrieval
  controls and CTD/metagenome shortcuts are hidden in the demo profile.
- Published dataset/assay combinations are selectable as complete setups.
  Fish-frequency and SST-comparison buttons apply the workflow, complete
  protocol, required sources and published SST dataset. Buttons replace filters
  explicitly; unavailable selections are not silently repaired.
- Free-text stays editable. The free-text button clears workflow and analysis
  pins and makes both supported evidence sources available.
- English/Japanese controls and preset questions are available.
- Figures and result tables precede the full answer. Methods, limitations,
  citations and diagnostic details remain expandable. Published numbers and
  denominators are preserved.
- Bounded English/Japanese paraphrases select the same immutable rows for the
  two demo workflows. Unknown terms, changed scope, unsupported metrics and
  qualifiers fail closed. Execution still validates cohort, source, publication
  and protocol constraints.

Professor examples E3-A/E3-B and E4-A/E4-B use the literal questions from the
October 21 email attachments. Real-data QA also checks Japanese equivalents,
exact row/citation parity, changed-year rejection and disabled-SST rejection.
The final ten-case job `ocean-cop17-acceptance-w4xhj` completed successfully;
its authenticated container timings ranged from 6.325 to 7.554 seconds.
The forthcoming presenter scenarios still need a final rehearsal.
