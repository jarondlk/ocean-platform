## Current contract and limitations

ScopeCompatibility(version, outcome, reason, workflow, analysis_id, evidence) and
ScopeEvidence(field, outcome, requested, registered, exact quote, source, reason)
are frozen dataclasses. as_dict serializes JSON-ready evidence lists. Contract
version is publication-scope-v1; the planner prompt version is auto-settings-v8.
Sources are question, selection and publication. Explicit unsupported filter keys
are rejected independently of their values. Whole-token attribute matching cannot
substitute NotMiSeq for MiSeq or 112S for 12S. Fixed spatial identity is resolved
from the existing canonical taxon key, not inferred from a vernacular name.

Geography aliases are a positive registered Miyagi set; other regions accept exact
registered IDs. Supported wording is bounded; unfamiliar scientific qualifiers
require clarification. C08/C19 may extend registered vocabulary/contracts with
regressions; they must not treat model readiness as proof. Spatial-year and
endpoint-year qualifiers select fixed result tables without narrowing the cohort.
Actual scientific artifact reliability/repair remains C02 and later gates.
