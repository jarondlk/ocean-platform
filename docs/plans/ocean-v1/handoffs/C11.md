## Limits and recovery

Trusted callers supply current whole-dataset access and scientific eligibility;
metadata does not grant review/publication. C14 retains authenticated service and
finer access enforcement. Rows are bounded at 2,000,000 and decoded bytes at
512 MiB across all SST objects in a snapshot, with C09's own bounds also applied.
Unsupported encodings, definitions, scopes and over-budget data fail explicitly.
Daily grid coverage means dates with at least one usable retained point, without
inferring complete spatial grid coverage. No production performance is claimed.

Actual source mappings/copies, scientific regeneration/review, publication
promotion, acquisition, service/UI integration and cloud access/deployment remain
outside C11's local acceptance. C02's affected real artifacts and C05's serving
rebuild remain deferred. Rollback disables additive SST exposure/registrations
while preserving sealed snapshots, receipts and current eligibility policy.
